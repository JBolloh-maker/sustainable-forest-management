Technology's Role in Modern Forest Management

Technology offers a powerful suite of tools to revolutionize forest management. Remote sensing via satellites and drones (UAVs) provides real-time data for large-scale, accurate monitoring of forest health, deforestation, and illegal logging.

Tools like LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) create precise 3D maps, enabling accurate estimation of biomass and carbon stocks. Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and AI-driven analytics process this vast data to predict fire risks, detect disease outbreaks, and optimize sustainable harvesting and replanting strategies. This allows foresters to make informed decisions quickly, enhancing both efficiency and sustainability.
